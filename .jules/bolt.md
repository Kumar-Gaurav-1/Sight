## 2024-05-24 - Avoid DateFormatter Initialization in Timer-Based Loops
**Learning:** `DateFormatter` is an expensive object to create. In `MenuBarViewModel.swift`, a new `DateFormatter` was being instantiated on every call to `updateDerivedProperties()`. Because `MenuBarViewModel` is bound to a timer state machine where `remainingSeconds` ticks down every second, `updateDerivedProperties()` was being called 60 times a minute. This frequent creation of a heavy `DateFormatter` for calculating the ETA (Expected Time of Arrival) caused unnecessary CPU overhead and performance degradation.
**Action:** Always extract expensive objects like `DateFormatter`, `ISO8601DateFormatter`, `NumberFormatter`, and `Calendar` to static or lazy properties, or class-level constants when they are needed within rapidly firing methods (such as timer callbacks or `updateDerivedProperties`).
