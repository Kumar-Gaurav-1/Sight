## 2024-11-09 - Caching DateFormatter instances
**Learning:** Instantiating `DateFormatter` or `ISO8601DateFormatter` in tight loops or frequently invoked methods (like UI rendering or CSV generation) is highly computationally expensive and causes redundant memory allocations, leading to measurable slowdowns. Caching them as globally shared objects reduces overhead significantly. In Swift 6 strict concurrency, they need proper `@unchecked Sendable` wrapper types or conditional `#if compiler(>=5.10)` annotations to avoid strict concurrency isolation compiler errors across isolated and non-isolated contexts.
**Action:** When working on Swift applications facing performance issues, look for redundant `DateFormatter` instantiations. Cache them safely in a globally accessible, properly annotated shared utility wrapper.
